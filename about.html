Cold Micro-Filtration

Our raw ingredients are processed using Cross-Flow Microfiltration (CFM) at strictly controlled cold temperatures. This high-technology isolation process strips away unwanted fats, cholesterol, and lactose while preserving valuable micro-fractions such as Alpha-lactalbumin and Immunoglobulins.
